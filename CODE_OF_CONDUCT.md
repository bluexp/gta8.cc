# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **GTA8.cc** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language (English and Chinese welcome).
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on accuracy and helpfulness for the GTA player community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting spam, malicious links, or deliberately falsified map coordinates.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `bluexp@126.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
